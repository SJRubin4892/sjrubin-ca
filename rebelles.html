How a practice night runs

Kids play first. Talking comes second.

Balls are out the moment they walk in. The parent conversation happens on the sideline while the players are already warming up — nobody stands in a line waiting to be told something.

6:45
Arrive, balls out, shooting.
7:00
Players start the warm-up. Parent talk runs alongside.
7:05
Warm-up — something loud and moving.
7:15
Skill block. Under thirty seconds of talk per drill.
7:35
Shooting. Form work close to the rim before anything competitive.
7:50
Small-sided games — constrain the game so the right decision is obvious.
8:10
Free throws in pairs, then one word each on what they learned.
