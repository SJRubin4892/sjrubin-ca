Drill Court

Fifteen drills animated on a half court in standard notation — solid line a run, zigzag a dribble, dashed arrow a pass — with the cue to say out loud for each one. Built to open on a phone at the gym.

Open the drill library

The shape of a practice night

6:45
Arrive, balls out. Nobody stands in a line waiting to be told something.
7:00
Kids start, parents talk. Agreed across all three coaches.
7:05
Warm-up — something loud and moving.
7:15
Skill block. Under thirty seconds of talk per drill.
7:35
Shooting. Form work close to the rim before anything competitive.
7:50
Small-sided games. Constrain the game so the right decision is obvious.
8:10
Free throws in pairs, then one word each on what they learned.
